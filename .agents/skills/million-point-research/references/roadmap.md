# Strategy selection

Read `docs/research/roadmap.md` and the relevant family in
`docs/strategies.md`. The default priority is:

1. reproduce and profile corrected fair D4;
2. accelerate exact search without changing its decisions;
3. complete the AFBR-40 successor-closure audit;
4. create action-complete, whole-origin-split, multi-cycle sibling data;
5. train a reflection-equivariant distributional afterstate ranker;
6. insert it as a confidence-gated correction above fair D4;
7. test human-inspired row-rise-cycle options as measurable abstractions;
8. revisit D5 and stochastic tree search after acceleration and leaf validation;
9. scale iterative all-sibling policy improvement only after offline gates pass;
10. qualify an unchanged candidate through fresh development, protected, and
    final cohorts.

The recurring failure to attack is sibling extrapolation: predicting visited
states well while ranking unplayed legal actions poorly. Prefer data and losses
that compare every legal sibling under aligned chance scenarios.

Treat stored chains, reservoirs, cover access, trigger reachability, phase, and
release timing as hypotheses with ablations—not as human folklore that bypasses
evaluation.

If no target-machine profile or authorized dataset manifest exists, begin with
the no-gameplay AFBR-40 closure preflight in `docs/research/roadmap.md`; do not
profile the wrong host or infer closure from task-record-only panels.
