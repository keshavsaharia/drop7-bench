---
title: Constructive and reservoir policies
summary: Don't wait for chains — build chain structures deliberately across row rises.
---

Most policies react to the board in front of them. Constructive policies try
to **manufacture** future chains: stack a high number where a later clear will
drop matching partners onto it, keep the trigger reachable, and survive the
rises in between. The standing structure is the "reservoir."

## What the evidence showed

- The constructive-spectrum planner materially beat fair D1 and remains a
  useful fast continuation — but applying it as an override on top of D4
  destabilized score, survival, and flow.
- A 12-move constructive horizon improved on a 7-move one, yet longer horizons
  were not monotonic. Planning further is not automatically planning better.
- The family verdict: a valuable **feature or option** inside a larger policy,
  not yet a standalone controller.
