---
title: Lifetime objective
summary: Optimize how long the game survives and how much flow it sustains, not just the next clear.
---

Score is a lagging indicator: the games that score a million points are first
the games that **refuse to die**. This family optimizes lifetime directly —
survival milestones, clear/reveal flow ceilings, risk calibration, and
score decomposition — on the theory that a policy which sustains the
approximately 2.4-clears and 1.4-reveals-per-move regime will collect chain
opportunities as they come.

## What the evidence showed

- Flow is a real constraint: policies that crack covers without sustaining
  reveals clog the board and die early, however good their tactics look.
- The family's preregistered 17k-scoring rollout-veto line is documented from
  its preregistration onward — see the approach pages for the current record.
- As with constructive reservoirs, lifetime signals look most promising as
  **bounded corrections** to a strong tactical fallback rather than as
  whole-game controllers.
