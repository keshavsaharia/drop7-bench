title: Afterstate learning
summary: Value the position a move leaves behind, not the move itself — with labels for every legal sibling.
---

The newest research line, built to break the sibling-extrapolation trap. An
**afterstate** is the board after a move resolves but before chance speaks
again. An action-free afterstate evaluator never sees which action produced
the state, so it cannot learn action-identity shortcuts — and a
**successor-closed** corpus labels *every* legal sibling, so deployment never
asks for an unsupported ranking.

## What the evidence showed

Three preregistered pilots (K=8, K=64, K=256 aligned chance scenarios) form a
model sequence of scientific records: two inconclusive label-stability
results, then a **valid negative** at K=256 — the calibrated model beat its D1
teacher but trailed fair D4 on every preregistered ranking metric in both
fresh held-out folds. A follow-up near-tie override gate produced the first
preregistered held-out improvement over D4 at all, yet still missed its frozen
margin. The full records are linked from the
[distributional-afterstate approach](/approaches/afterstate-learning/distributional-afterstate)
and the [experiments page](/experiments).
