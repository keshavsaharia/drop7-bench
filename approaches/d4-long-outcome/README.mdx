summary: Teachers built from exact D4 search and 25-move outcomes — and the veto that almost worked.

Fair D4 is strong but sees only four plies. This family asks what lies beyond:
label root actions by their **long outcomes** (25-move continuations, terminal
results), then distill, rank, or veto.

## The headline experiment

The exact D4 + 25-move D2 rollout veto let a costly common-tape comparison
overrule fair D4 only when the long evidence disagreed. One pilot reached a
**404,047-point** mean with quality gates passed — but it missed its runtime
limit by a wide margin, and the frozen replication later failed its gates.
Signal existed; it did not survive contact with confirmation.

## What the evidence showed

- Long-outcome labels carry real signal that D4 cannot see (rise-boundary
  fragility, slow-burn structure).
- Distilling that signal into a fast student kept failing sibling-ranking
  gates — the same trap documented in
  [value and policy learning](/approaches/value-policy-learning).
- A cheaper exact veto that preserves the original intervention decisions is
  still an open direction.
