summary: The reference line — search that averages chance honestly instead of hoping or fearing.
---

Expectimax treats Drop7 as what it is: a game of alternating **choices** and
**chance**. At choice nodes it takes the best column; at chance nodes — the
next disc, the gray reveals — it takes the probability-weighted average. That
fairness is the whole idea, and the ledger shows it beats optimistic,
worst-case, and under-sampled chance handling.

## The landscape

This family holds the reference policy and its close variants:

- **The reference itself** — completed full-width depth 3 (D3) and depth 4
  (D4) with the fair leaf. D4 is the strongest dependable public policy found
  so far.
- **Leaf tuning** — the fair evaluator's coefficients were fitted by sweeps
  and CEM over complete games.
- **Depth and chance variants** — selective D5, full D5, seven-stratum chance
  coverage, root risk shaping. All were rejected or runtime-paused: more
  depth did not reliably mean more strength.
