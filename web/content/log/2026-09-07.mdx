## Training on the boards the search evaluates

Yesterday's fill experiment measured the gap between the boards the tables
were trained on and the boards the deployed search reads: about nine
tenths of the entries never updated, concentrated on full lines, and the
one repair that moved the depth-3 leaf without training was a change to
the price of exactly those unfamiliar boards. The owner asked for the
obvious next thing: train the leaf on the boards the search evaluates, not
just the ones one-ply play visits.

Experiment `EX-20260907-ntuple-treestrap-warm-start-c33ba880`, run
`RUN-20260907T012358Z-a00ecdf0`, branch `claude/n-tuple-search-targets`.
A training-time copy of the deployed depth-3 seven-stratum search plays
every training move over the current tables. After each move the board the
game is on is trained toward the search's own value of it, an expectation
over seven reveal strata and three plies instead of one sampled step; and
in the arm the experiment is named for, every internal node of the search
tree, the boards one and two imagined moves from the root, about 2,250 per
decision, is trained toward its own backed-up value. Chess programs have
trained linear evaluations this way under the name TreeStrap. The search
decides by a utility that adds a million-point penalty at death, and
training toward that number would fold the penalty into the tables, so the
training search carries two values per node, the utility that picks the
move and the score-only value of the same decisions, and trains toward the
second. Both arms warm-start from the frozen tables with fresh step sizes,
as the fill experiment's arms did. A second arm, the ablation, uses the
same actor and root target and touches no internal node.

<Finding title="The training search is the engine's search, bit for bit" kind="neutral" metric="120 sampled states: 0 value mismatches, 0 decision mismatches; 2,226 internal targets per decision">
The new gate compares the training search's root column values at depth 3
against the engine's own on 120 live sampled states with the frozen tables
as the leaf, and its decisions against the engine's deployed decision with
iterative deepening and the work bound: every value equal bit for bit,
every decision equal, two decisions on the same state identical, and the
collected internal targets finite, at depths 1 and 2, at most 2,450. The
smoke run on the probe block measured 540 to 630 searched moves per second
on 32 threads with about 2,250 internal targets per decision, about two
thousand times fewer visited moves per second than one-ply play and about
the same number of table updates per second.
</Finding>

Each arm validated the warm start itself before any training move, then
every three hundred thousand searched moves, on a fresh 256-game block
(`0xa52f2a80`); training read a fresh block at `0xa5a00000`.

<Finding title="Both arms fell below the warm start at once and never recovered" kind="negative" metric="warm start +174,466 on the validation block; best points +140,529 (visited states only) and +93,767 (every tree node)">
On the validation block the frozen tables are 174,466 ahead of the fair
leaf. The visited-states arm read +101,920 at its first point, wandered
between +72,053 and +140,529, and stopped by the plateau rule at 1.8
million searched moves after six points. The every-tree-node arm read
+10,059 at its first point, with one-ply play down from 284,964 to 182,700,
climbed to +93,767 at its seventh point and ran to the 100-minute wall at
3.1 million moves and ten points, 7.2 billion node updates in all. Neither
arm's best point reached the warm start, so the pilot-tier training-signal
check reads not passed for both. The shape of both curves is the shape of
a shock: a fresh coherence accumulator lets every entry's first update run
at the full step, the internal-node targets differed from the tables'
values by about eleven rise units at the start, and the search actor makes
far too few visited-state updates per second for the annealing that healed
the fill experiment's one-ply control within seconds.
</Finding>

<Finding title="A measurable loss at both depths, and the internal nodes made it worse" kind="negative" metric="-108,970 at depth 3 (bounds -139,543 to -78,715); -101,253 at depth 4; ablation -73,150 and -77,543">
The TreeStrap candidate as the depth-3 leaf averaged 375,372 against
484,342 for the frozen tables on the same games, 108,970 behind with the
upper bound below zero, 206 wins to 306, both halves negative and a lower
quartile 22,685 lower: every criterion of the gate fails and the loss is
measured, not merely unresolved. The ablation lost 73,150, and the
candidate lost a further 35,819 to the ablation (bounds minus 64,276 to
minus 7,292), so training the internal nodes hurt on top of the search
targets at visited states. At depth 4 the candidate is 101,253 behind and
the ablation 77,543 behind. All five readings beside the gate are refuted.
The fourth ply was worth about the same to every table (47,932 to the
frozen tables, 55,648 to the candidate, 43,539 to the ablation), and the
frozen tables' depth-3 margin over the fair leaf replicated on this fifth
block at 163,499 (lower bound 133,843). The one-ply continuation from
yesterday, replayed here, is 17,867 below the frozen tables with bounds
either side of zero.
</Finding>

<Direction title="What the record supports next" status="proposed" owner="claude-fable-5-1 (Claude Code)">
The configuration rejected is the warm start at the full step with fresh
step-size state, and the validation curves say the step is the problem:
one-ply training with the same fresh start healed its first-update shock
in seconds in the fill experiment, and the search actor cannot. The
successor, preregistered and launched the same morning, runs the same
TreeStrap and visited-states arms at alpha 0.05 and 0.2, with a 2,048-game
depth-3 screen so the floor for the primary contrast is about half of
today's. If the gentle step does not beat the warm start on validation
either, training toward the search's own backups from this warm start
will have been tested at three step sizes, and the n-tuple leaf's
training-target axis joins scale and fill conditioning as tried without a
measurable gain at this budget.
</Direction>
